## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: Sleek developer launch film — dark mode, glowing accents, fluid motion, authentic dev momentum.
- Interpretation: Fast-in transitions with steady holds for readability, authentic developer UI components, high-contrast dark theme.
- Angle: Turning solo coding fatigue into collaborative momentum by connecting developers with open-source teams and co-founders in real time.
- Hook: A minimal terminal-style prompt "Still coding alone?" followed by the glowing invitation "Find your co-builders."
- Outro / punchline: The illuminated ProjectBuddy brand emblem, wordmark, and "Build together. Ship faster."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language ("Streamline your workflow")
  - Abstract filler visuals or generic 3D shapes
  - Overly busy or unreadable text animations

## Storyboard
Total Duration: 19.5s
1. **The Hook** (0.0s – 3.5s) — OLED black screen. Prompt "Still coding alone?" pops in, then pill badge "Find your co-builders. Ship your dream projects." glows into place.
2. **The Project Discovery** (3.5s – 8.0s) — Glassmorphic "DevPulse AI" card slides in with tags (`Next.js`, `TypeScript`, `Tailwind`), team status (`2 of 4 joined`), and needed roles. Simulated cursor clicks "Request to Join Team" at 7.5s.
3. **The Match & Team Chat** (8.0s – 13.0s) — "Request Accepted 🎉" toast drops in. Real-time chat bubbles pop in with Alexchen welcoming the builder and double blue ticks (`✓✓`) confirming delivery.
4. **The Developer Ecosystem** (13.0s – 16.5s) — 3 stat/feature pillars appear sequentially across musical beats: Open Source Teams, Real-time Chat, Co-Founder Match.
5. **Outro & Logo Payoff** (16.5s – 19.5s) — Glowing ProjectBuddy Monogram Buddy emblem scales in with wordmark, "Build together. Ship faster.", and `projectbuddyapp.vercel.app`.
